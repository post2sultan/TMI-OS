# Campaign Radar 2.0

Radar 2.0 treats URLs, articles, videos, images, feeds, and observations as
signals. Signals are grouped into campaign candidates; only corroborated or
owner-confirmed candidates become campaigns.

## Locked delivery path

- RADAR-04: signal and campaign-cluster separation
- RADAR-05: reusable watchlists and Arabic/English query planning
- RADAR-06: multi-source collectors and scheduled monitoring
- RADAR-07: local entity matching, clustering, confidence, and trend scoring
- RADAR-08: candidate-centric command-center dashboard

## Later intelligence concepts

- Saudi brand universe and relationship graph
- Campaign DNA and reusable creative fingerprints
- Competitive creative maps and share of voice
- Trend velocity, burst detection, and early-signal forecasting
- Outdoor photo intake with OCR, location, and visual matching
- Executive daily intelligence briefs and governed alerts

All milestones remain local-first, source-attributed, human-governed, and free
of paid AI dependencies by default.
