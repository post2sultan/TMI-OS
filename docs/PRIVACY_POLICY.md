# TMI OS Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

## 1. Overview

TMI OS is an owner-operated, locally hosted application. This policy explains the information used when the owner connects social-platform accounts and publishes approved content.

## 2. Information processed

Depending on enabled integrations, TMI OS may process:

- OAuth identifiers, access tokens, refresh tokens, and account or channel identifiers;
- basic connected-account information returned by a platform;
- campaign source material, generated scripts, captions, hashtags, audio, video, and approval status;
- local operational records needed to validate and troubleshoot publishing.

## 3. Purpose of processing

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, create and review campaign content, upload or publish owner-approved content, display publishing status, and maintain local operational reliability.

## 4. Storage and security

Platform credentials are stored locally in a Windows user-bound encrypted vault with restricted file permissions. Credentials and vault backups are excluded from Git. TMI OS does not intentionally publish credentials or place them in repository files.

## 5. Sharing

TMI OS sends only the information required to the platform selected by the owner. Information is not sold. Connected platforms process submitted information under their own privacy policies and terms.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Local campaign data and generated assets remain on the owner's machine until the owner removes them. Platform access can be revoked through the relevant platform's account settings. Local credentials can be replaced or deleted from the encrypted vault by the owner.

## 7. User controls

The owner controls which accounts are connected, which campaigns are approved, and when content is uploaded or published. Requests concerning content on a third-party platform should also be handled through that platform's controls.

## 8. Children's privacy

TMI OS is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes

This policy may be updated when product behavior or platform integrations change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

## 10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be submitted through the [TMI OS GitHub repository](https://github.com/post2sultan/TMI-OS).
