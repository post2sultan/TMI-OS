# TMI OS media brand lock

- Navy `#034C6B` is the dominant field and overlay colour.
- White `#FFFFFF` is the primary text colour.
- Orange `#F37F17` is limited to a small accent line or marker.
- Red `#EB1924` is exceptional-only and is not used in routine overlays.
- The supplied TMI logo is mandatory as a watermark on every video.
- Noto Sans Bold/Medium is the production-safe overlay typeface; Noto Sans
  Arabic is used for Arabic text.
- Stock media provenance is retained in each campaign's `media-manifest.json`.
