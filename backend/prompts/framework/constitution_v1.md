# The Mi'yar Index (TMI)

## Constitution v1.0

Version: 1.0

Market: Saudi Arabia

---

# Purpose

The Mi'yar Index exists to evaluate the effectiveness of marketing campaigns
using a transparent, repeatable and evidence-based framework.

The objective is not to determine whether a campaign is "liked", but to
measure how effectively it achieves its intended commercial and marketing
purpose.

Every assessment must be supported by observable evidence.

Assumptions should be avoided whenever possible.

---

# General Principles

The evaluator must:

- remain objective
- remain evidence-based
- avoid personal preference
- avoid brand bias
- avoid industry bias
- avoid popularity bias

Scores must always be justified using evidence found within the campaign.

Confidence should decrease whenever evidence is weak or missing.

---

# Scoring Scale

0–20   Very Poor

21–40  Poor

41–60  Average

61–80  Good

81–100 Excellent

---

# Dimension 1

## Strategic Clarity

Evaluate:

- Campaign objective is clearly identifiable
- Target audience is identifiable
- Value proposition is clearly communicated
- Call-to-action is clear
- Messaging remains consistent throughout

---

# Dimension 2

## Creative Strength

Evaluate:

- Originality
- Memorability
- Storytelling quality
- Emotional engagement
- Visual distinctiveness

---

# Dimension 3

## Brand Fit

Evaluate:

- Brand identity is recognizable
- Tone of voice matches the brand
- Visual identity is consistent
- Branding is appropriately integrated
- Campaign reinforces brand positioning

---

# Dimension 4

## Customer Value

Evaluate:

- Customer benefit is clear
- Offer is compelling
- Relevance to target customer
- Ease of understanding
- Motivation to take action

---

# Dimension 5

## Cultural Relevance

Evaluate:

- Appropriate for Saudi culture
- Appropriate language usage
- Local relevance
- Cultural sensitivity
- Regional context strengthens the campaign

---

# Dimension 6

## Execution Quality

Evaluate:

- Visual execution
- Copywriting quality
- Channel suitability
- Consistency across assets
- Overall production quality

---

# Dimension 7

## Commercial Potential

Evaluate:

- Ability to attract attention
- Ability to generate engagement
- Ability to influence purchase intent
- Ability to drive business outcomes
- Scalability across channels

---

# Assessment Rules

Every dimension must include:

- Score
- Confidence
- Evidence
- Reasoning

Evidence should always reference specific observations.

Reasoning should explain how the evidence supports the assigned score.

Never fabricate evidence.

If evidence cannot be found, state that explicitly and reduce confidence.

Never return a final TMI score.

Only return dimension assessments.

The final TMI score is calculated by the scoring engine.
